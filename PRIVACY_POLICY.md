*   **Personal Data:** Name, phone number, and profile picture (if provided) for account registration.

## 3. How We Use Information

*   To manage user accounts and provide support.
*   To send notifications about the status of requests.

## 5. Data Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
